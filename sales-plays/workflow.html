Qualify

Owner AE · with the BDR

  1. Make contact with the lead

    The BDR creates and qualifies the lead.

  2. Intro call: business discovery AE

    Why, why now, budget, and who decides.

    What to cover
    • Why? Why now? Is there budget?
    • Understand the decision process
    • Identify everyone involved, from IT and the business
    • Use the first-call deck to find where we fit

Bring in if…

It's a strategic initiative

Bring in APEX (CTO office, formerly BIG) and a Field CTO for C-suite alignment and product vision. Bigger deals start here. Request an Expert

A partner is in the account

Engage the partner early; implementation partners already work closely with the customer. Start with your Partner Sales Manager (PSM), who aligns the partner and co-sell. Requests for a specific partner go through its Slack workflow: #accenture-requests, #snowflake-requests, #rsm-requests, #connorgroup-requests. AWS Marketplace private offers: #aws-mpopp-requests.

2

Discover

Owners SE + AE

  1. Technical discovery SE

    Turn use cases into business value, and agree the next step.

    What to cover
    • Validate why and why now
    • Uncover the technical use cases
    • Map each use case to business value
    • Agree the next step: demo, workshop or POC

  2. Match it to a sales play

    Find an active play, with its decks and demos, in the Sales Play Library.

  3. Technical validation document

    Write down the use cases and their value; confirm with the customer.

    How
    • Start from the template
    • Outline the use cases for the demo, workshop or POC, mapped to business value
    • Fill it in, then confirm it with the customer
Still qualified?No → end

Bring in if…

You need domain expertise

Request an Expert: Domain Experts by domain (Data Management incl. BDI and Elementary · iPaaS & B2B/EDI · APIM · AI Connectivity & Governance · Platform Solutions), Field Engineers (SE use) or the AI CoE / AI Solution Architects.

Security or architecture comes up

Security questions: #boomi-security. Architecture boards and roadmap: a Field CTO or Field Engineer via Request an Expert.

3

Prove

Owner SE · AE aligned

  1. Demo SE

    Start with DemoHub; request a custom build only if needed.

    Options
    • DemoHub: short videos and clickables you can send ahead
    • Tailored demo or asset: Request an Expert → New Demo Assets, or Custom Demo/PoC
    • Environment access: Demo Environment Access

  2. AI lab or workshop

    Request an Expert → Workshop/Lab, with the AI CoE for in-depth AI.

  3. POC

    Submit a POC Request once five entry criteria are met.

    The five entry criteria
    1. Success criteria defined (confirmed by the SE)
    2. Sales leadership aligned with the executive buyer; project is a business priority (RSD validates)
    3. Customer gives access to its systems and people
    4. Level of effort from Boomi confirmed
    5. Agreement to move to commercial terms on success (AE and RSD validate)
Technical win?No → end

Value & close

Owner AE · SE supports
Business case needed?No → sizing

  1. Build the business case AE

    Quantify ROI for the executive buyer.

    Tips
    • Quantify ROI and value for the executive buyer
    • Reuse the play's value messaging where one exists

  2. Sizing discussion

    SE and AE size the platform, with the ESM for services.

    Who and what
    • SE and AE size the platform
    • Bring the ESM (Enterprise Solutions Manager, PSO Sales) for professional services, hosted runtime or a Customer Care bundle

  3. Proposal AE

    RVP, ESM, SE and AE aligned on the offer.

    Before you send
    • RVP, ESM, SE and AE aligned on the offer
    • Check the Sales Play Library for active play offers
